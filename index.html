“Good writing doesn't always need to be loud. Sometimes it needs to be useful. Sometimes it needs to make you laugh. And sometimes, it just needs to make you feel understood.”

Professionally, I work with content, communication, and creative projects. Personally, I'm drawn to the little things people overlook: the things we say without thinking, the things we feel but never explain, and the humour hidden in ordinary life.

I'm building a body of work that brings strategy and storytelling together — with curiosity, clarity, and a little bit of tamasha.
